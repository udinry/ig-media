# Privacy policy: ig-owner (Desi Degen channel tool)

ig-owner is a private command-line tool that Udbhav Naryani uses to publish and manage his own content on his own YouTube channel (Desi Degen) and Instagram accounts. It has no other users and no public sign-up.

**What it accesses:** only the owner's own channel, through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, after the owner signs in with Google OAuth. That covers video uploads and metadata, channel and video statistics, and comments on the owner's own videos.

**Storage:** the data is stored only on the owner's computer, to measure and improve the channel's content. It isn't sold, shared or shown to anyone else. Statistics older than 90 days are deleted.

**Third parties:** none. No data is sent to anyone other than Google's own APIs.

**Revoking access:** the owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool also follows the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
